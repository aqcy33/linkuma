Linkuma isn't trying to be everything. A few things to know upfront:

- **No major media placements** — if you need links from high-traffic news sites or ultra-high-authority domains for competitive niches like finance or insurance at scale, you'll likely want to combine Linkuma with other platforms.
- **French market roots** — the platform is expanding internationally, but its strongest network depth remains in European markets.

These aren't dealbreakers for most campaigns. They're just honest trade-offs at this price point.
